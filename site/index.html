Money in, money out, and who owes whom

An offline expense tracker made in Nepal. Everything you need for daily kharcha, and nothing you have to learn.

  • Rent, salary and other bills that repeat add themselves on the due day.
  • Set a monthly budget and a limit for any category.
  • Move money between cash, bank and eSewa without counting it as spending.
  • Nepali rupees by default. Any other currency works too.
  • Lock it with your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Back up to one file, or export a spreadsheet for Excel.

The helper runs on your own phone or computer. Download it once, 2 to 4 GB depending on the device, and it works with no internet. Your voice, photos and entries never leave the device.

  • Type it. "tea 60", "salary 50000", "lent Ram 2000".
  • Say it. Tap the mic and say "khana 450". Nepali and English both work.
  • Snap it. Take a photo of a bill. It fills in the amount, date and shop.
  • Ask it. "How much did I spend on food this month?" or "Who owes me money?"

Nothing is saved until you tap Save, and Undo puts it back. In a browser the helper can chat, but voice and photos need the app. Phones with less than 6 GB of memory may be slow.

Questions

Is Spendrix really free?

Yes. There's no paid plan and no ads.

Do I need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. You only need the internet to download the AI helper once, and to sync if you turn it on.

Where is my data kept?

On your device. If you turn on sync, your entries are locked on your device before they leave, and only your devices hold the key.

What if I lose my phone?

Turn on sync, or back up to a file from Settings now and then. Either way you can bring everything back on a new phone.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Not yet. Until then you install the file from GitHub. Every release there is built from the public code.

How do I update?

Spendrix shows a card on Home when a new version is out. Install the new file over the old app. Don't uninstall first, that deletes your data.

Does it work in Nepali?

The AI helper understands Nepali and English, typed or spoken. The app's screens are in English for now, and new users start with Nepali rupees.

Which devices can run the AI helper?

Most 64-bit Android phones, iPhones, Macs with Apple chips, and 64-bit Windows and Linux PCs. In a browser it needs Chrome or Edge. Phones with less than 6 GB of memory may be slow. Everything else in Spendrix works without it.

I used Spendrix in my browser before. Where did my entries go?

Nowhere. The web app moved to spendrix.web.app/app/. Open it in the same browser and your entries are right there.
