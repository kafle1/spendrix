Spendrix is a private money diary. It works offline, keeps everything on your device, and has an AI helper that runs on your phone or computer.

**Downloads**
- Android: `Spendrix-android.apk`. Older 32-bit phones use `Spendrix-android-32bit.apk`, and Intel Chromebooks use `Spendrix-android-x86_64.apk`. Neither of those can run the AI.
- Android, if you have Spendrix 1.2 or older: `Spendrix-android-for-1.2-and-older.apk` (or `Spendrix-android-32bit-for-1.2-and-older.apk`). Those versions were installed under an older name, so only this file updates them in place.
- Windows: `Spendrix-windows.zip`, unzip and run `spendrix.exe`
- macOS: `Spendrix-macos.dmg`. The app isn't signed yet, so right-click it and pick Open the first time.
- Linux: `Spendrix-linux-x64.tar.gz`, extract and run `./spendrix`
- iPhone: `Spendrix-ios-unsigned.ipa`, sideload only (AltStore or Sideloadly) until it is on the App Store
- Web: open https://spendrix.web.app, or host `Spendrix-web.zip` yourself

**Updating from 1.x**
- Install the new file over the old app. Don't uninstall first, that deletes your data.
- Your accounts, categories, entries, lend records and spending limits move over on first launch. Lend records you had marked settled show up as paid back.
- If Spendrix opens empty and asks you to set it up, it was installed next to the old app instead of over it. Install the other Android file, which updates the old app and keeps your data. Then open both Spendrix icons and uninstall the one that's empty.

**What's in it**
- Add money out, money in, transfers, and money you lent or borrowed in a few taps
- Just talk to it. Type, or tap the mic and say "khana 450" or "बिजुली बिल ११००", and it drafts the entry for you to save
- Take a photo of a bill right in the chat and it fills in the amount, date and shop
- Ask questions like "how much did I spend on food last month?"
- Bills that repeat add themselves
- Charts for where your money goes, month by month
- Optional sync between all your devices. Everything is encrypted on your device first, so nobody else can read it, us included.
- Backup to a file, export to a spreadsheet, dark mode
- Lock the app with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN

**Good to know**
- If you forget your sync password, your synced copy can't be recovered. Your data on each device stays safe.
- Photos stay on the device they were taken on.
- The AI is a one-time download of about 2.6 GB (2 GB in the browser), then it works offline. In the browser it can chat but can't hear you or read photos.
